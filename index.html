The person behind the cuts

I’m Nasrudeen — I go by Nasru Editz. I’m a freelance video editor who thinks about a video the way an audience actually watches it: the first second, the reason to stay, and the reason to share.

My edits lean on storytelling and audience psychology as much as they lean on software — pairing cinematic visuals with refined pacing, motion graphics, seamless transitions and sound design that carries a viewer to the last frame.
